The Hollow Crypt Privacy Policy

Dragonium LLC · Effective November 9, 2026

This policy explains how The Hollow Crypt ("the game"), made by Dragonium LLC ("we"), handles your information.

We don't collect your data

The game does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal or usage data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet while you play.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress, settings and play statistics in files on your own device. These files never leave your device and we cannot see them. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes them.

Permissions

  • Vibrate: lets the game make your device vibrate. It gives the game no access to your information.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Third parties

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information under its own privacy policy when you download or update apps; see Google's Privacy Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If the game ever starts collecting data, this page will say so before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our site's issue tracker.
